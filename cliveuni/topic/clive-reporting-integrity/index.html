Unknown Stays Unknown Until the Data Exists

Clive Collective operating story

10 min3 lessons2 checks

Clive operating example — sanitized

Lessons

Challenge

Polished-looking reports were being produced even when current-period source data was incomplete or missing. The reports looked authoritative but could not be verified against actual operating numbers. The risk was that decisions would be made on partial information presented as complete.

Unknown Stays Unknown Until the Data Exists — approved Clive University case-study source

Move

The reporting standard was changed: when current-period source data is missing, the report now explicitly marks that data as unknown rather than filling gaps with estimates or prior-period numbers. Missing data blocks the production of a polished report until the source information is available.

Unknown Stays Unknown Until the Data Exists — approved Clive University case-study source

Operating Lesson

Management integrity means unknown stays unknown. A report that looks complete but contains unverifiable numbers is worse than no report at all, because it creates false confidence. The discipline is to surface gaps rather than paper over them.
