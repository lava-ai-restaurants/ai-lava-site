Opportunity exercise: Repeatable opening/closing standards and local guest feedback

Design a repeatable opening checklist and closing checklist for Neon Spur. The checklists should cover equipment readiness, station setup, cleanliness verification, and cash/POS procedures. Then design a simple guest feedback mechanism that captures actionable information without requiring technology the location does not have.

Neon Spur — approved Clive University field-guide source

Deliverable

An opening checklist and a closing checklist, each with specific verification steps. A guest feedback capture method with a plan for weekly review of collected feedback.
