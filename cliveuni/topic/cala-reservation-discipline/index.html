Fixing Cancellation and No-Show Execution

Cala operating story

10 min3 lessons2 checks

Clive operating example — sanitized

Lessons

Challenge

Cancellations and no-shows were handled inconsistently. Confirmation at check-in was not always performed correctly. When guests cancelled, visit notes were not reliably recorded. Seating decisions after cancellations lacked shared ownership among the management team.

Fixing Cancellation and No-Show Execution — approved Clive University case-study source

Move

The team established correct confirmation procedures at check-in, ensuring every arriving party is verified against the reservation. For cancellations, managers now record visit notes documenting the cancellation and any relevant context. Seating responsibility after cancellations became a shared management function rather than falling to whoever happened to notice the empty table.

Fixing Cancellation and No-Show Execution — approved Clive University case-study source

Operating Lesson

Reservation discipline is a system, not a reaction. Correct confirmation at check-in, documented cancellations, and shared seating responsibility prevent small gaps from compounding into lost revenue and poor guest experience.
