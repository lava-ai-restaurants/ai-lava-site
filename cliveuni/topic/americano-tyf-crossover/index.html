Cross-Concept Guest Journey Thinking

The Americano + Tell Your Friends operating story

10 min3 lessons2 checks

Clive operating example — sanitized

Lessons

Challenge

Late-night guests at Tell Your Friends and dinner guests at The Americano operated as separate audiences with no designed connection between the two concepts, even though they share a building. The opportunity to move guests between concepts was not being captured systematically.

Cross-Concept Guest Journey Thinking — approved Clive University case-study source

Move

A Friday and Saturday reverse happy hour was designed to create a guest pathway from Tell Your Friends upstairs to The Americano. The program was structured to give late-night TYF guests a reason to transition to the Americano dining experience. This is an example of cross-concept guest-journey thinking: designing an operating connection between two venues rather than treating them as independent.

Cross-Concept Guest Journey Thinking — approved Clive University case-study source

Operating Lesson

When multiple concepts share proximity, the guest journey between them is a design problem, not an accident. Strategic operating connections between venues can serve guests better than treating each concept as isolated. This remains a strategic operating example; outcomes depend on continued execution.
