Manager lab: Reservation discipline and shift accountability

Review the current reservation confirmation process at check-in. Map every step from guest arrival through seating. Identify where confirmation gaps or handoff failures could occur. Then review the food-runner check-in/check-out process and dry-storage signoff controls.
