Ideal vs. Actual: The Report That Finds Your Money

The ideal vs. actual product usage report compares two numbers: what you should have used based on what the POS says you sold, and what you actually used based on inventory counts. The gap between these two numbers tells you where product is disappearing. If the POS says you sold 40 orders of salmon this week, and your recipe card says each order uses 7 ounces, the ideal usage is 280 ounces. If your inventory shows you actually used 340 ounces, that 60-ounce gap represents waste, portioning errors, unreported spoilage, or theft. Without this report, you know your food cost is high but you do not know why.

Based on David Scott Peters food management software methodology

Key Insights

With this report, you can identify exactly which products are over-used and investigate the cause. The report requires three inputs: accurate recipe costing cards, reliable POS data, and consistent inventory counts. If any of these inputs is unreliable, the report will be misleading. This is why recipe cards and inventory discipline must come first. The ideal vs. actual report is the payoff for building those foundational systems.
