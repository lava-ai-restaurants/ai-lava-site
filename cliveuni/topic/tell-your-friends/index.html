Manager lab: Handoff to Americano and event follow-through

Map the current guest experience at Tell Your Friends on a Friday or Saturday night. Identify the moment where a guest could transition to The Americano and what currently happens at that moment. Review event follow-through processes: what happens after an event is booked, confirmed, executed, and closed.
