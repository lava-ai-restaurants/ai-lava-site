Calculate, track, and reduce food cost every week

Calculating Actual Food Cost

Actual food use equals beginning inventory plus purchases minus ending inventory. Most restaurants fail because they don't calculate food cost properly or frequently enough. Weekly tracking is the foundation of cost control.

4 Steps to Calculate Restaurant Food Cost

Tracking Daily Sales by Category

Track gross sales by category -- food, bottle beer, draft beer, wine, liquor, and merchandise. This breakdown feeds your weekly prime cost and budget variance report, giving managers the data to calculate food cost accurately each week.

Why You Should Track Daily Sales in Your Restaurant

Budget Variance and Manager Ownership

When managers present their own food cost numbers and identify variances, the dynamic shifts: instead of the owner interrogating, the manager owns the problem. A manager who says 'I found the problem, I fixed it, and here is what remains' is operating at a higher level.

Understanding and Utilizing a Weekly Restaurant Budget Variance Report

Knowledge Check

What is the formula for actual food use?

  • Total sales minus total purchases
  • Beginning inventory plus purchases minus ending inventory
  • Ending inventory minus beginning inventory
  • Total purchases divided by total sales

Why should managers present their own food cost numbers at weekly meetings?

  • It saves the owner time on data entry
  • It shifts accountability so the manager owns the problem and the solution
  • It is only required by health code
  • It replaces the need for inventory counts

Field Assignment

Pull this week's beginning inventory, purchases, and ending inventory. Calculate actual food cost percentage. Identify the top three dollar variances and bring them to your next weekly review with a proposed corrective action for each.
