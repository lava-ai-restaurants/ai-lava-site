Opportunity exercise: Opening-readiness discipline and source-of-truth launch checklist

Build a source-of-truth launch checklist for a new restaurant opening. The checklist should cover staffing readiness, menu finalization, equipment verification, vendor setup, training completion, and operating system setup. Each item should have a clear owner and a verification method. This is a planning exercise, not a timeline commitment.

Kuza — approved Clive University field-guide source

Deliverable

A launch-readiness checklist with categories, specific items, assigned ownership, and verification criteria. The checklist should be usable as an ongoing status tracker during the opening process.
