Managing a Major Menu Transition

Bungalow Kitchen Tiburon operating story

10 min3 lessons2 checks

Clive operating example — sanitized

Lessons

Challenge

A significant menu transition required coordination across manager logs, staff training, revised service standards, and operating stabilization. The scope of the change meant that multiple systems needed to update simultaneously, and the risk of inconsistency during the transition period was high.

Managing a Major Menu Transition — approved Clive University case-study source

Move

The management team used structured manager logs to track the transition. Training sessions covered the new menu items and revised service standards. Operating stabilization became an explicit goal with defined checkpoints rather than an assumed outcome. The transition is being managed as an ongoing process with active monitoring.

Managing a Major Menu Transition — approved Clive University case-study source

Operating Lesson

Major menu transitions are operating projects, not just kitchen events. They require manager logs, training plans, revised service standards, and explicit stabilization checkpoints. Treating a menu change as a one-day event creates weeks of inconsistency.
