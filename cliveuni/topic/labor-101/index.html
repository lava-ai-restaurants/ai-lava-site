Labor Cost and Scheduling

Schedule on budget, track hours and dollars, and control overtime

15 min2 lessons2 checks

Lessons

The Restaurant Payroll Guardian

Control labor costs by scheduling for both dollars and hours, not just filling shifts. Give your management team the ability to know exactly what people should be working before the schedule goes live. Scheduling software that shows sales forecasts alongside labor spend is essential.

Master Restaurant Kitchen Systems for Seamless Operations

Scheduling on Budget Each Week

Set a labor budget to hit your ideal labor cost for the year. Use scheduling software so you can see your sales, hours, and dollar amounts before you schedule. Go into each week on budget by building the schedule against a target, not reacting after payroll runs.

Essential Tactics to Control Restaurant Labor Cost

Knowledge Check

What should you see in scheduling software before publishing a schedule?

  • Only employee availability
  • Sales forecasts, scheduled hours, and projected labor dollars
  • Just the number of shifts filled
  • Only the previous week's actual hours

What is the Restaurant Payroll Guardian approach?

  • Hiring a dedicated payroll clerk
  • Scheduling for both dollars and hours against a budget target
  • Cutting staff after every slow night
  • Outsourcing all scheduling to corporate

Field Assignment

Before publishing next week's schedule, calculate your labor budget target. Compare projected hours and dollars against that target. Identify any day where projected labor exceeds the budget and adjust before the schedule goes live.
