Training and Systems for Manager Accountability

First learn, then train your managers on your system, your process, your way. Step-by-step procedures that define how things get done are the foundation. Then put systems in place to hold managers accountable to those standards.

Restaurant Owner Success Depends on This

Knowledge Check

What must happen before you can hold a manager accountable?

  • Threaten consequences for poor performance
  • Define the job, the method, and the required standard, then train to it
  • Simply tell them to do better
  • Wait until the end of the quarter to review

What shifts when foundational operating systems are in place?

  • The owner can work on budgets, marketing, and team development instead of firefighting
  • The restaurant no longer needs managers
  • Food cost automatically drops to zero
  • The owner never needs to visit the restaurant

Field Assignment

Choose one recurring management task (e.g., daily line check, weekly inventory count, nightly close procedure). Write a clear step-by-step standard for it. Train the responsible manager this week and schedule a verification checkpoint.
