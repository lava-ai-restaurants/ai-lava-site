Delegation and Trust

Stop doing everything yourself and develop the next generation

15 min2 lessons2 checks

Lessons

Trust and Verify

Let managers struggle and figure things out. They may surprise you. Your job is to develop the next generation of leaders. If you keep doing everything yourself, you will never step back, and the restaurant will never reach its full potential.

How Restaurant Owners Can Make Sure Their Team Can Handle It

Implementing One System at a Time

Pick any system you can handle -- whether standardizing a bar drawer count, recipe costing cards, shelf-to-sheet inventory, scheduling on budget, or ideal vs. actual usage. Start with one, implement it, and build from there rather than trying to change everything at once.

How to Manage a Restaurant 5 Tips from Successful Independent Owners

Knowledge Check

What does 'trust and verify' mean in delegation?

  • Trust blindly and never check results
  • Delegate the task, let them work through it, then verify the outcome
  • Do the work yourself and have someone watch
  • Only trust senior managers with any task

Why should you implement one system at a time?

  • Multiple systems are always contradictory
  • It is easier to build consistency, train properly, and verify results before adding complexity
  • There is only one system that matters
  • Managers cannot learn more than one thing per year

Field Assignment

Identify one task you currently do yourself that a manager could own. Write out the process, hand it off this week, and schedule a verification check-in within 7 days. Document what you observe.
