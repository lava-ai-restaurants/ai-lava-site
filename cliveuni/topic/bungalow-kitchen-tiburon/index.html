Manager lab: Menu-transition consistency and manager log adoption

Review the current state of manager log usage at Bungalow Kitchen. Identify whether logs are being written consistently, whether they contain actionable information, and whether incoming managers read them. Then examine one aspect of the recent menu transition and assess whether service standards have stabilized.
