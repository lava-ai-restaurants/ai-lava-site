Inventory and Recipe Costing

Recipe cards, shelf-to-sheet inventory, and ideal vs. actual usage

15 min2 lessons2 checks

Lessons

Why Recipe Costing Cards Matter

Without recipe costing cards, you cannot know your true cost per plate. While manual cards are time-consuming and quickly outdated, food management software keeps recipe costs current by syncing invoices, inventory, and menu mix automatically.

Restaurant Solutions for Independent Restaurants

Ideal vs. Actual Product Usage

The most powerful output of food and beverage software is ideal versus actual product usage. Because you take inventory, enter invoices, maintain recipe costs, and tie in POS data, you can see exactly where usage exceeds what should have been used -- and investigate the gap.

Kitchen Systems Every Restaurant Needs to Use to Lower Food Cost

Knowledge Check

What is the main advantage of software-based recipe costing over spreadsheets?

  • Spreadsheets are more accurate
  • Software automatically updates recipe costs when invoice prices change
  • Spreadsheets update in real time
  • Software eliminates the need for inventory

What does 'ideal vs. actual product usage' reveal?

  • How much product you ordered
  • The gap between what should have been used based on sales and what was actually used
  • The total cost of your menu
  • How many recipes you have entered

Field Assignment

Pick your top five selling menu items. Verify that each has a current recipe costing card with accurate ingredient prices. For any item missing a card or with outdated pricing, build or update it this week.
