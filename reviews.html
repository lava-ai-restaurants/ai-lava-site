The Problem

You have a 1-star review from last week you haven't responded to. You don't know your average rating trend. A guest complained about cold food and you found out on Yelp instead of from your manager.
  • Reviews sit unanswered for days — or forever
  • You check Google sometimes, Yelp occasionally, and forget OpenTable exists
  • No system to identify patterns — the same complaint repeats for months
  • Great staff mentions in reviews go unnoticed and unrecognized

How We Fix It

Our AI monitors every review platform — Google, Yelp, OpenTable, TripAdvisor — and aggregates them into one daily digest. Every morning, you know exactly what guests said about you last night. Response time drops from days to hours.

Sentiment analysis identifies patterns humans miss. If 6 reviews in 30 days mention "long wait for drinks," we don't just tell you — we correlate it with staffing data to find the root cause. Negative reviews trigger escalation alerts so they're addressed immediately.

We also extract the good stuff. Staff name mentions from positive reviews get pulled automatically for recognition programs. Monthly review reports show trends, improvement areas, and wins — all in one place.

This is the lowest-cost service we offer because it delivers the highest ROI per dollar. One bad review handled well is worth more than any ad campaign.

What This Looks Like

Sample scenarios based on typical engagements.

Review Aggregation
Reviews were scattered across Google, Yelp, OpenTable, and TripAdvisor. Nobody was responsible for monitoring all of them. Average response time: 4 days.
Aggregated all platforms into one daily digest. Average response time went from 4 days to 4 hours. No review goes unread or unanswered.
Pattern Detection
Identified a pattern: 6 reviews in 30 days mentioned "long wait for drinks." Correlated with scheduling and staffing data.
Investigation revealed the bar was understaffed Friday/Saturday 7-9pm. Added one bartender to peak hours. Drink wait complaints dropped to zero within 2 weeks.
Staff Recognition
Extracted 23 staff name mentions from positive reviews in one month. Most managers had no idea which employees were being praised by guests.
Used for a recognition program that highlighted top performers monthly. Staff morale improved visibly and turnover reduced 15% over the following quarter.
